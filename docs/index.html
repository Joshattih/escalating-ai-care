Escalation rules explorer

Every rule a clinician, counsellor, judge or developer is told to follow, taken from 14 official severity and escalation rating systems (2007 to 2026). One rule per row; each traceable to its document and page. A rule is a statement telling the actor what to look for, how to classify what they found, or what to do about it. A rule describes a state when it says something about the patient at a moment; only state rules become statements for rating. Hover a yes or no in the State column to see the note on a close call.
