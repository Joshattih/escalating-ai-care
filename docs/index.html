The rules baseline

Three tables, one chain. A rule is a line in an official document that tells the clinician what to look for, how to classify it, or what to do (773; in the repository). A state is a rule that says something about the patient at a moment (259). A statement is a state after cleaning, and it is what the AI doctors rate (257): every statement describes one patient at one moment, in the source's own window, with the source's verdict removed, so that any disagreement recorded is a disagreement between doctors about a patient and not about what a sentence meant. Each statement keeps its row id, document and page, and the people its guideline says it applies to (the "Applies to" column, from populations.json) and the place its guideline was written for (the "Setting" column, from settings.json; the seven settings are listed in the repository's data folder).
